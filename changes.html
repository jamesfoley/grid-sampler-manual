Grid Sampler manualChanges

Changes

What's new in each update, newest first.

8 October 2026

  • MULTI: a page after STRETCH that turns one note into a multisample. It renders the sample at a note every few semitones across the range you pick, each at its own pitch but the original length, and swaps them in as zones, so a note plays at its proper length on every key. UNDO puts the single sample back. The folder it saves loads into any slot with Spread on ZONES.
  • Spread on ZONES loads its samples at the action's QUALITY, and gives each zone the action's loop, direction, tuning, level and pan.
  • ZONES shows which zones are sounding as you play, and names their samples.
  • QUALITY on the SAMPLE page: ORIGINAL, HIGH (mono), MEDIUM (mono mu-law), LOW and LO-FI (at half and a quarter the rate), from full size down to a sixteenth of the memory, for the action's sample and its zones. The card keeps the original, so it's reversible, and the low tiers have that early-sampler grit.
  • The page marker in the header is a bar along its bottom edge rather than a square per page, leaving room as pages are added.

7 October 2026

  • Time stretch: a STRETCH page after CHOP stretches START..END to another tempo without changing its pitch, and swaps the result in for the sample; the original file stays on the card. CLEAN keeps every hit tight, stretching only the tails between them. CYCLIC is the old Akai way, repeating short grains, with the metallic jungle sound. UNDO puts the original back.
  • Resampling: Shift + REC opens SAMPLING, which records the main out or one mixer channel into a new sample. It can start on the push, on the first sound over a threshold, or at the top of the pattern, and stop after a number of bars. The take is trimmed, saved to /recordings and put on the next note you play.
  • Two individual outputs: OUTPUT on a channel's STRIP page and on each bus page sends it to MAIN, OUT 3, OUT 4 or OUT 3+4. OUT 3 and 4 need the second DAC of the next hardware.
  • Shift + push TRACK on SEQ's TRACKS page deletes that track from the pattern. UNDO brings it back.
  • The screen is laid out as one row of eight columns, one per encoder. A box above a column shows the setting in full while you turn or push it, and values too long for a column scroll.
  • The mixer's knobs work as a console row: PREV and NEXT step all eight through LEVEL, PAN, SEND A, SEND B, MUTE and SOLO.
  • On and off settings are drawn as switches, and choices such as filter type as small pictures.
  • Knob labels sit at the left of their boxes, with rounded tops.
  • The browser opens at the root of the card, and recordings and stretches are saved in /recordings and /stretched there.
  • A little colour: what Shift changes is shown in yellow while it's held, and recording shows in red.
  • The knobs are arranged more consistently. ADD and ACTION come first on SLOT. Envelopes sit on knobs 5 to 8 on both AMP and FILTER. RETURN, PAN and OUTPUT stay in the same columns on both bus effects, and the channel compressor matches the main out's. REVERSE moves to LOOP, MEMORY to PLAY (the SYSTEM page is gone), and the zone KEYS setting to INPUTS as ZONE KEYS. Zones get a GAIN knob. On the piano roll, MARKER (to add a note) comes first, and on TRACKS, SLOT does. On naming, DONE is knob 7 and CANCEL knob 8.

6 October 2026

  • Long samples start more reliably when several start at once, and sequenced notes keep better time.

5 October 2026

  • The sequencer: patterns, recording and overdubbing with a count-in, a piano roll to edit notes, and UNDO for the last edit.
  • ERASE, NOTE REPEAT, swing, quantise, TAP tempo, and MIDI clock out on TRS.
  • An arpeggiator on each slot, and scales and keys for slots played across a range.
  • Delay SYNC to the tempo, a smoother limiter on loud mixes, and Shift + push to reset any setting.
  • The browser has modes, and a project can be started empty.
  • Up to four USB MIDI devices through a hub.
  • CC learn: turn a setting, press LEARN, move a controller knob. Shift + LEARN lists the bindings.
  • Arrangements: patterns laid out on a timeline and played stacked, with moving, copying, naming and playing from any bar.

4 October 2026

  • The mixer: eight channels with filter, drive, bitcrush and compressor, level, pan, width, mute, solo and two sends; a delay and a reverb bus; EQ, compressor and limiter on the main out.
  • Any MIDI device and note can play a slot: one note, a pitched range or stepped levels.
  • Projects are saved to the card and loaded back, and slots and projects can be named.
  • The ZONES page: several samples across the keys, each with its own markers, loop, tuning and level.
  • Samples load when a project needs them and free themselves once nothing uses them. MEMORY on SETUP frees and tidies sample memory without reloading.
  • SETUP, with the memory in use and a 30 second stress test, and a SYSTEM page to keep a slot's samples whole in memory.
  • The SLOTS list, deleting slots, and deleting projects from the browser.
  • Marker knobs speed up when turned fast, and the header shows how many voices and streams are playing.
  • Fixed notes sticking when a device is unplugged, and presses during loading.

3 October 2026

  • The display runs at its full 480 by 320.

2 October 2026

  • Chopping a sample onto pads, editing a sample while it plays, and scrolling the waveform.
