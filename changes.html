Grid Sampler manualChanges

Changes

What's new in each update, newest first.

10 October 2026

  • The key that opens SLOTS is now called SLOTS (it was EDIT).
  • STOP stops a sample previewing in the browser.
  • The header shows the whole device name instead of cutting it at 11 letters.
  • SCROLL's bar shows where the zoomed waveform really is: it no longer starts in the middle and jumps when turned.
  • The playhead moves about twice as smoothly, and pages draw faster.
  • Long samples streaming from the card no longer drop out, and sequenced notes no longer arrive late, when a busy project keeps the processor working hard.
  • Voices, effects and the output limiters use less of the processor, so busy projects have more room.
  • The STRESS TEST on SETUP runs everything at once: voices, streams, a playing pattern with the click, every channel's inserts, FX and sends, the line in, the individual outputs, beat repeat and a recording. It reports late notes as well as the processor's peak and dropouts.

9 October 2026

  • A new look: dark ink on a warm paper ground, in new typefaces (Space Mono, Space Grotesk). THEME on SETUP switches between PAPER and DARK, kept with the other device settings.
  • A screen's key goes straight to that screen from any other, without closing the one open first.
  • QUALITY's choices show how much memory the sample takes at each. MULTI's ZONES and SIZE, which no knob changes, are shown greyed. EDIT's GAIN only shows for the GAIN edit.
  • QUANTISE is now QUANT, and STRENGTH is AMOUNT.
  • The mixer is strips, as on a console: the eight channels, RETURN A and B, and MAIN, each with its own pages. The faders' last row, RETURNS, has the returns' and the main out's levels; push one to open it. Shift + PREV and NEXT step through every strip. A channel's pages no longer end with the buses and the main out.
  • Effects are one set, used the same way everywhere: a channel's FX, a send bus and MAIN FX all pick from the same list with the same knobs. A send bus can now be any effect, a shared chorus, tape or tremolo as well as DELAY or REVERB; MAIN FX can be any a channel can. Projects saved before load as they were.
  • The new panel: four rows of keys and eight knobs. Transport on the left with SHIFT bottom left; PREV, NEXT and the screens (SLOTS, MIXER, SEQ, KIT, BROWSE, SETUP) on the right. Press an open screen's key again to close it.
  • New Shift combinations: Shift + REC overdubs, Shift + PLAY taps the tempo, Shift + BROWSE opens SAMPLING (was Shift + REC), Shift + KIT opens CCS (was Shift + LEARN), Shift + SETUP saves, and from SETUP it saves as.
  • The keys light up: SHIFT yellow while held, PLAY green while playing, REC red while recording, and the open screen's key blue.
  • FX on each mixer channel, a page after INSERTS: one effect per channel from TAPE (saturation, age, wow, flutter, hiss), VINYL (narrow band, warp, crackle, surface noise), CHORUS, FLANGER, PHASER and TREMOLO (level or pan).
  • CCS: each bound CC gets MIN and MAX (the part of the range it covers, or turned round), CURVE (LINEAR, EXP, LOG) and MODE: ABSOLUTE, PICKUP (waits until the knob reaches the setting, so nothing jumps), RELATIVE (endless encoders) or FINE (14-bit, CC n with CC n+32).
  • Beat repeat: hold Shift + REPEAT and the main out loops its last slice, as long as REPEAT's note value on the SEQ REC page; let go and it carries on live.
  • FILTER on the MAIN FX page: a DJ filter on the whole mix, low-pass turning left, high-pass turning right, off in the middle.
  • ROWS on the PLAY page: with a SCALE set, FOURTHS lays the notes out as a grid with each row of 8 pads a fourth above the last, as Push does, so a Launchpad spans about 4 octaves instead of 9.
  • A INTO B and B INTO A on the SENDS page: one send bus's effect on into the other, such as the delay's repeats into the reverb. One way at a time.
  • MAIN FX, a mixer page after MAIN OUT: TAPE or VINYL on the whole mix, after its level.
  • SYNC on TREMOLO, CHORUS, FLANGER and PHASER: RATE becomes a note value from 1/16 to 8 bars, and while the sequencer plays the sweep is locked to the beat.
  • Line in on any channel: INPUT on a channel's STRIP page brings in the line input (stereo, or one side), so an external synth plays through that channel's effects, sends and outputs, and SAMPLING can record it.
  • MIDI in on the TRS jack: notes and CCs arrive from the device TRS IN, so a keyboard or drum machine can play slots and LEARN can bind its knobs.
  • DUCK on each channel's COMP page: the channel dips while another plays, for the pumping sidechain sound.
  • Four DACs: the main out and six individual outputs, OUT 3 to OUT 8, each alone or in pairs (3+4, 5+6, 7+8).
  • OUTPUT on SETUP sets every output's level, 0dB down to -60dB, kept with the other device settings. It replaces PHONES and LINE OUT, which belonged to the old audio board.
  • Pushes that delete something are labelled in red.
  • The SD card can be pulled out and put back: taking it out stops what's playing and closes the browser, and putting it back mounts it again within a second.
  • Starting up shows a GRID SAMPLER splash instead of a flash of whatever was on the screen before it was switched off.
  • The panel goes dark while the instrument starts, and keys pressed during start-up are ignored.
  • The browser says when a file or folder name is over 63 characters, or a path over 191, instead of picking something that won't play.
  • Fixed: a long sample whose full path on the card was over 95 characters played its first moment, then stopped. Paths up to 191 characters now work everywhere.
  • Fixed: pushing ADD on an empty note did nothing if the last slot was left on its INPUTS, PLAY or ARP page.
  • A Launchpad is now just an 8x8 grid with slot LEDs. Its edge buttons no longer stand in for the panel; they send ordinary CCs you can LEARN.

8 October 2026

  • Long sample, slot and device names in a knob column scroll all the way to their end instead of stopping at 12 characters.
  • EDIT: a page after MULTI to trim, discard, silence, gain, normalise, reverse or fade the part between START and END, or snap the markers to zero crossings. Each edit is saved as a new file and swapped in, and UNDO puts it back.
  • MULTI: a page after STRETCH that turns one note into a multisample. It renders the sample at a note every few semitones across the range you pick, each at its own pitch but the original length, and swaps them in as zones, so a note plays at its proper length on every key. UNDO puts the single sample back. The folder it saves loads into any slot with Spread on ZONES.
  • Spread on ZONES loads its samples at the action's QUALITY, and gives each zone the action's loop, direction, tuning, level and pan.
  • ZONES shows which zones are sounding as you play, and names their samples.
  • QUALITY on the SAMPLE page: ORIGINAL, HIGH (mono), MEDIUM (mono mu-law), LOW and LO-FI (at half and a quarter the rate), from full size down to a sixteenth of the memory, for the action's sample and its zones. The card keeps the original, so it's reversible, and the low tiers have that early-sampler grit.
  • The header names the pages either side of the one shown, so you can see where PREV and NEXT go. It replaces the marker bar.

7 October 2026

  • Time stretch: a STRETCH page after CHOP stretches START..END to another tempo without changing its pitch, and swaps the result in for the sample; the original file stays on the card. CLEAN keeps every hit tight, stretching only the tails between them. CYCLIC is the old Akai way, repeating short grains, with the metallic jungle sound. UNDO puts the original back.
  • Resampling: Shift + REC opens SAMPLING, which records the main out or one mixer channel into a new sample. It can start on the push, on the first sound over a threshold, or at the top of the pattern, and stop after a number of bars. The take is trimmed, saved to /recordings and put on the next note you play.
  • Two individual outputs: OUTPUT on a channel's STRIP page and on each bus page sends it to MAIN, OUT 3, OUT 4 or OUT 3+4. OUT 3 and 4 need the second DAC of the next hardware.
  • Shift + push TRACK on SEQ's TRACKS page deletes that track from the pattern. UNDO brings it back.
  • The screen is laid out as one row of eight columns, one per encoder. A box above a column shows the setting in full while you turn or push it, and values too long for a column scroll.
  • The mixer's knobs work as a console row: PREV and NEXT step all eight through LEVEL, PAN, SEND A, SEND B, MUTE and SOLO.
  • On and off settings are drawn as switches, and choices such as filter type as small pictures.
  • Knob labels sit at the left of their boxes, with rounded tops.
  • The browser opens at the root of the card, and recordings and stretches are saved in /recordings and /stretched there.
  • A little colour: what Shift changes is shown in yellow while it's held, and recording shows in red.
  • The knobs are arranged more consistently. ADD and ACTION come first on SLOT. Envelopes sit on knobs 5 to 8 on both AMP and FILTER. RETURN, PAN and OUTPUT stay in the same columns on both bus effects, and the channel compressor matches the main out's. REVERSE moves to LOOP, MEMORY to PLAY (the SYSTEM page is gone), and the zone KEYS setting to INPUTS as ZONE KEYS. Zones get a GAIN knob. On the piano roll, MARKER (to add a note) comes first, and on TRACKS, SLOT does. On naming, DONE is knob 7 and CANCEL knob 8.

6 October 2026

  • Long samples start more reliably when several start at once, and sequenced notes keep better time.

5 October 2026

  • The sequencer: patterns, recording and overdubbing with a count-in, a piano roll to edit notes, and UNDO for the last edit.
  • ERASE, NOTE REPEAT, swing, quantise, TAP tempo, and MIDI clock out on TRS.
  • An arpeggiator on each slot, and scales and keys for slots played across a range.
  • Delay SYNC to the tempo, a smoother limiter on loud mixes, and Shift + push to reset any setting.
  • The browser has modes, and a project can be started empty.
  • Up to four USB MIDI devices through a hub.
  • CC learn: turn a setting, press LEARN, move a controller knob. Shift + LEARN lists the bindings.
  • Arrangements: patterns laid out on a timeline and played stacked, with moving, copying, naming and playing from any bar.

4 October 2026

  • The mixer: eight channels with filter, drive, bitcrush and compressor, level, pan, width, mute, solo and two sends; a delay and a reverb bus; EQ, compressor and limiter on the main out.
  • Any MIDI device and note can play a slot: one note, a pitched range or stepped levels.
  • Projects are saved to the card and loaded back, and slots and projects can be named.
  • The ZONES page: several samples across the keys, each with its own markers, loop, tuning and level.
  • Samples load when a project needs them and free themselves once nothing uses them. MEMORY on SETUP frees and tidies sample memory without reloading.
  • SETUP, with the memory in use and a 30 second stress test, and a SYSTEM page to keep a slot's samples whole in memory.
  • The SLOTS list, deleting slots, and deleting projects from the browser.
  • Marker knobs speed up when turned fast, and the header shows how many voices and streams are playing.
  • Fixed notes sticking when a device is unplugged, and presses during loading.

3 October 2026

  • The display runs at its full 480 by 320.

2 October 2026

  • Chopping a sample onto pads, editing a sample while it plays, and scrolling the waveform.
