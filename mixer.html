5.1 MIXER faders

MIXER.

Eight channels, a fader and a knob each. The knobs work as a console row: PREV and NEXT step every knob through LEVEL, PAN, SEND A, SEND B, MUTE and SOLO together, and the header says which.

KnobSettingTurnPush
1 to 8CH 1 to CH 8That channel's LEVEL, PAN, SEND A, SEND B, MUTE or SOLO, whichever row is pickedOpens that channel's pages. Shift + push resets the row's setting on it

Knobs not listed do nothing here. Shift + push puts most settings back to how a new one has them.

5.2 MIXER, PAN row

NEXT on the faders.

The same faders with the row on PAN. NEXT again gives SEND A, SEND B, MUTE and SOLO.

5.3 Channel: STRIP

Push a channel's knob on the faders.

One channel's own pages. PREV and NEXT step its pages, Shift + PREV and NEXT step channels, and pushing knob 8 goes back to the faders. The top lists the slots playing through it.

KnobSettingTurnPush
1LEVELThe channel's level in dB
2PANLeft to right
3WIDTHStereo width, 0 (mono) to 100%
5MUTESilences the channelToggles
6SOLOHears only the soloed channelsToggles
8OUTPUTMAIN, OUT 3, OUT 4 or OUT 3+4. On OUT 3 or OUT 4 alone, left and right are summed to mono. The individual outputs skip the main out's EQ, compressor and MASTER, and have a limiter eachBack to the faders

Knobs not listed do nothing here. Shift + push puts most settings back to how a new one has them.

5.4 Channel: INSERTS

NEXT from STRIP.

KnobSettingTurnPush
1FILTEROFF, LOW, HIGH or BAND
2CUTOFFThe filter's cutoff
3RESOIts resonance
5DRIVESaturation, 0 to 100%

Knobs not listed do nothing here. Shift + push puts most settings back to how a new one has them.

5.5 Channel: COMP

NEXT from INSERTS.

A compressor after the inserts. It's off at a RATIO of 1:1.

KnobSettingTurnPush
4MAKEUPGain after it, 0 to 24dB
5THRESHWhere it starts, -60 to 0dB
6RATIOHow hard, 1:1 to 20:1
7ATTACK0.5 to 200ms
8RELEASE10ms to 2sBack to the faders

Knobs not listed do nothing here. Shift + push puts most settings back to how a new one has them.

5.7 BUS A and B

NEXT from SENDS (BUS A), then BUS B.

The two send busses, the same from every channel. Each shows only its effect's settings: here a reverb. BUS B, below, is a delay.

KnobSettingTurnPush
1EFFECTOFF, DELAY or REVERB
2SIZE / TIMEReverb: the room's size. Delay: the delay time up to 2s, or with SYNC on a note value from 1/32 to 1/1
3FEEDBACKDelay: how much repeats, up to 95%
4DAMPDarkens the reverb's tail, or each repeat
5SYNCDelay: TIME follows the tempo as a note valueToggles
6RETURNThe level back into the mix
7PANWhere the return sits
8OUTPUTWhere the return plays: MAIN, OUT 3, OUT 4 or OUT 3+4, as a channel's OUTPUTBack to the faders

Knobs not listed do nothing here. Shift + push puts most settings back to how a new one has them.

5.8 MAIN OUT

NEXT from BUS B.

The main output: a 3-band EQ, its level, then a compressor.

KnobSettingTurnPush
1LOWLow shelf, -12 to +12dB
2MIDMid band
3HIGHHigh shelf
4MASTERThe output level
5THRESHThe compressor's threshold
6RATIOIts ratio
7ATTACKIts attack
8RELEASEIts release

Knobs not listed do nothing here. Shift + push puts most settings back to how a new one has them.
